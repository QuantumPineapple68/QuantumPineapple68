Solo-designed AI-powered social platform for real-time skill exchange, with automated NSFW moderation, AI-driven certificate validation, and semantic skill-matching over a vector DB. Launched globally, **4.7★**, and the subject of a published international research paper.
`Java` `Firebase` `Cloudflare Vectorize`
